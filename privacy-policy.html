KuroWav Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026
Operator: KuroWav (independent software project)
Contact: kurowavpng@gmail.com

1. What KuroWav does

KuroWav is a Windows PNGTuber application. It lets you display and animate images, respond to microphone input, create avatar profiles, and optionally display speaking indicators and PNGs for people in a Discord voice call when authorized access is available.

2. Information the application processes

  • Avatar and project data: PNG image files you choose, layer settings, motion and rigging parameters, hotkeys, friend-sprite assignments, and other settings needed to display your character.
  • Microphone input: KuroWav analyzes live microphone sound levels to animate the avatar's mouth and movements. Microphone monitoring may start when the application launches; you can switch it off in the application or revoke microphone access in Windows. The current application is not designed to transcribe speech or retain recordings of your conversations.
  • Optional Discord data: If you separately authorize a supported Discord connection, the application may process Discord user identifiers, display names, voice-channel participation and speaking-state events for the purpose of displaying and animating friend sprites. Discord voice permissions may not be available without Discord approval. KuroWav does not need your Discord password.
  • Local preferences and diagnostics: The application can save the selected microphone, window positions, last opened project, Discord application ID and crash diagnostics needed to troubleshoot technical issues.

3. Where information is stored

KuroWav is currently designed primarily for local use. Profiles and preferences are stored on your own computer, typically in %LOCALAPPDATA%\KuroWav\ on Windows, or at another location you choose when saving a project. Project files may contain friend names, Discord user IDs and references to your PNG files.

The current application does not provide an operator-run account system or cloud synchronization service. Its local voice-event bridge accepts authorized events on your own device. We do not intentionally collect microphone audio recordings, chat message contents, or Discord account passwords on an operator-controlled server.

4. Third-party services

If you use the optional Discord feature, Discord handles its own sign-in and authorization processes under its policies. KuroWav's ability to receive voice events depends on permissions Discord grants. If you visit our public policy pages hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical information about your visit under GitHub's own privacy terms. Your streaming platform or OBS setup is also governed by its own terms.

5. Sharing and disclosure

KuroWav does not intentionally upload your PNG files, microphone audio, or saved profiles to a KuroWav-operated cloud service in the current release. Information may be shared if you deliberately export or distribute a project, share files, or broadcast avatar output. We may disclose information if legally required, to the extent any such information is held by us.

6. Retention and your choices

You can stop microphone monitoring in KuroWav, disable its Discord connection, and delete local profiles and preferences. Removing the application does not necessarily remove files you saved separately. You can find local profiles under %LOCALAPPDATA%\KuroWav\Profiles and preferences at %LOCALAPPDATA%\KuroWav\settings.json. For questions about data we might hold, contact us using the address above.

7. Security and limitations

KuroWav uses local application storage and authorization mechanisms where applicable. No software can guarantee absolute security. Only connect Discord through an authorization prompt that you recognize and do not share account tokens or passwords.

8. Children and regional rights

KuroWav is not directed specifically at children. Depending on where you live, you may have additional legal rights concerning personal information. Contact us for help exercising rights relating to information actually held by the KuroWav operator; local files are controlled directly by you.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if KuroWav's functionality or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated. Material changes should be reflected in the application or on the official policy page.
